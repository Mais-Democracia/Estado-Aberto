# Data licence

This repository distributes no data. The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers code, schemas, synthetic sample generators and documentation only.

Data produced by running the ingest tools comes from public sources. Each source keeps its own licence and terms of use, listed per source in `docs/SOURCES.md`. Whoever runs the tools is responsible for complying with those terms, including robots.txt and rate limits, and for how they reuse the resulting data.

Synthetic sample data generated by `tools/make_sample.py` is invented, says nothing about real people or bodies, and is released under the same MIT licence as the code.

See `docs/DATA_POLICY.md`.
